Spider 2.0, a text-to-SQL / data-analysis benchmark. Its questions supply the
history for the session-analysis task in [[actor-gatherer-framework-blog]].

## Facts
- Session analysis asks what was done in a history of past data-analysis
  questions "based on Spider 2.0 results" (e.g. "which questions discussed
  Africa"), scored by F1 of the returned set. AG-Codex is annotated
  "+0.19 F1" over the best baseline ([[actor-gatherer-framework-blog]]).
