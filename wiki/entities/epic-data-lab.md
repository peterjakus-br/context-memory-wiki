GitHub organisation is `ucbepic`.

- The blog footer reads "Developed at the EPIC Data Lab, UC Berkeley". The
  page also carries the logos of UC Berkeley, EPIC and the Sky lab
  ([[actor-gatherer-framework-blog]]).
- Publishes the code at `github.com/ucbepic/actor-gatherer` and the site at
